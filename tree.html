Animal Tree

Everyone carries a code like calm young bear. The animal is the nature, read from three small tests: strong but stiff, flexible, strong and flexible, steady balance, all-rounder or gentle start, with three animals to choose from in each. Its age is the level, from baby to ancient, and grows through feedback on the poses. The first word is the pace, from calm to swift. Theme and length are picked fresh every day. 6 natures × 5 ages × 3 paces = 90 positions.
